THE CORE

A data centre dug under the factory. Pod columns of docked turtles stand in a great hall, wired to spines of modems and chests, lit green from behind the panels. Every structure here is a real blueprint: the same data feeds these renders, the Create schematics and the builder turtles.

    In the repo and in the game

    Files

    design/structures.mjs every structure, defined once
    node design/generate.mjs --install writes blueprints and schematics, and copies the schematics into the game
    schematics/core_*.nbt in the ATM10 instance: load them in Create's Schematic Table
    programs/blueprints/*.lua what the builder turtle builds
    builder stack on a turtle at a blueprint's home, supply chest below, staging chest above
    datapack/core_factory_chisel makes every Factory Block variant chiselable (server)
