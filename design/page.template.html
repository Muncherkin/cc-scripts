A proposal for works beneath the factory · All the Mods 10

The Clockworks

A works dug sixty blocks under the factory. Dark hives of berthed turtles stand in a great hall, piped to spines of modems and chests and joined by iron-railed bridges, all of it in grey plating and green vents. Every structure is a real blueprint: the same figures drive these plates, the Create schematics and the builder turtles.

Plate VI

Order of works

Iron sets the pace: every 32 Factory Blocks cost 4 iron and 4 stone, and 16 iron bars cost 6 iron. The costs are counted from the blueprints.

    Plate VII

    Materials

    Factory Blocks in dark greys and green, and heavy iron bars for every railing. Variants come from plain Factory Blocks at a Mason Table, or through a chisel once the datapack is in.

    Factory Blocks give no light. The green panels stand where lamps would go; the halls stay dark, and mobs can spawn in them, until light is added.

    Appendix

    Where the drawings are kept

    design/structures.mjs every structure, set down once
    node design/generate.mjs --install writes the blueprints and schematics, and copies the schematics into the game
    schematics/core_*.nbt in the ATM10 instance, for Create's Schematic Table
    programs/blueprints/*.lua what the builder turtle builds
    builder stack on a turtle at a blueprint's home: supply chest below, empty chest above
    datapack/core_factory_chisel makes every Factory Block variant chiselable (server)
