Juniper farms 4 pines east of the factory: about 24 logs a round, and a round is a few minutes. Every log is a charcoal for the crew and the furnaces.

## Step 1: 12 spots
Same turtle, same program: it takes its spots as arguments, so a bigger field is only a restart with a longer list.
```
 x  0  4  7 10 13
 z0 F
 z2    S  S  S  S
 z5    S  S  S  S
 z8    S  S  S  S
```
F factory, S a pine. Three blocks apart, like now.
- 3x logs | ~72 a round
- 8 Pine Sapling | it keeps 32
- 0 new parts
Juniper digs snow down to the soil and steps up a block where the ground rises. It flies over anything else and never cuts wild trees.

## You check
- Ground | x 3-14, z 1-9 east of F
- Nothing built there
- Soil under the snow | dirt or grass
Then I restart it with the 12 spots.

## Step 2: Pots below
In the Conservatory, Hopper Botany Pots grow pines with no light and no turtle: 1-4 logs per pot every 2 minutes, about 75 an hour each. They drop into chests on the factory network.
- 8 pots | ~600 logs/h
- 8 Hopper Botany Pot
- 8 Dirt
- 8 Pine Sapling
- 8 Chest
- 8 Wired Modem
The apple pots from the Golden Orchard go beside them. Juniper can then retire, or keep the surface grove for saplings.
