# Tree Station
> Botany Pots + crafty turtle (draft)
```
 [pot][pot][pot][pot]
 [hop][hop][hop][hop]
   |    |    |    |
   +----+----+--> chest
 ~~ wired network ~~
 furnace fuel storage
   [crafty turtle]
```
Pots grow birch and push drops into hoppers. A crafty turtle sorts everything over a wired network.

## It will make
- Charcoal | fuel for every turtle
- Chests | deposit chests for the miner
- Torches | sticks + charcoal
- Bone meal | from surplus saplings, for the wheat farm

## Check first
- 1 Hopper Botany Pot | does it exist in JEI?
- Time one birch | how long until logs?

## Materials (rough)
- 6 Hopper Botany Pot
- 6 Hopper
- 6 Dirt
- 6 Birch Sapling
- 1 Crafty Turtle | turtle + crafting table
- 1 Furnace
- 1 Composter
- 2 Hopper | above and below the composter
- 5 Chest
- 8 Wired Modem
- 32 Networking Cable
