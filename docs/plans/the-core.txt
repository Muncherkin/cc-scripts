# The Clockworks
> Works beneath the
> factory
Columns clad in turtles stand in a great hall 60 blocks down: strips five wide, the full height, each turtle on a modem wired to a spine of chests. Bridges five wide, railed with andesite bars, join them, the Mainspring and the Bridge. Stairs join every deck. Walls are one dark block with a scattering of similar ones.

## Places
- The Engine Hall | 49 wide
- Hives A-D | 320 turtles each
- The Mainspring | gear tower
- The Bridge | command room
- The Ledger Room | storage
- The Hatchery | births
- The Conservatory | tree bay
- The Lift | turtle-9 elevator
- The Pneumatic Gate | ore in
- The Pattern Shop | chisels

## Hive, from above
```
    PP.....PP
   PMM.....MMP
  PMM       MMP
  .M         M.
  .B    S    B.
  .M         M.
  PMM       MMP
   PMM.....MMP
    PP.....PP
```
P turtles (four strips), M their modems, S the spine, . the open ring round it.

## Costs
- 122 Iron Ingot | one Hive
- 1454 Iron Ingot | the whole works
4 iron + 4 stone make 32 Factory Blocks.

## How it gets built
The builder turtle digs out a blueprint and places every block. Start it on the blueprint's home with a supply chest below and an empty chest above.
- builder stack | one hive
- builder fields | the hall
Schematics for Create are in the game's schematics folder as core_*.nbt.

## Phases
- Pattern Shop or Mason Table
- Dig the Engine Hall
- Hive A, the Mainspring
- The Bridge
- The Ledger Room
- The Conservatory
- Hives B-D, the Hatchery

## Light
Factory Blocks give none. Green panels mark where lamps would go; add light or mobs spawn.
