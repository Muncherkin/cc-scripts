# The Clockworks
> Works beneath the
> factory
Columns clad in turtles stand in a great hall round the foot of the lift, 85 blocks down: strips five wide, the full height, each turtle on a modem wired to a spine of chests. The columns are sealed: only turtles go in. A stair in the Mainspring and a bridge railed with andesite bars lead up to the Bridge. Walls are one dark block with a scattering of similar ones.

## Hive, from above
```
    PP.....PP
   PMM.....MMP
  PMM       MMP
  .M         M.
  .B    S    B.
  .M         M.
  PMM       MMP
   PMM.....MMP
    PP.....PP
```
P turtles (four strips), M their modems, S the spine, . the open ring round it.

## Costs
- 122 Iron Ingot | one Hive
- 1454 Iron Ingot | the whole works
4 iron + 4 stone make 32 Factory Blocks.

## How it gets built
First the excavator digs out the whole works, 60,400 blocks, on its own. Place it at the top of the lift shaft on its east side, facing the shaft, with a chest behind it. Give it a bucket and 32 coal blocks.
- factory birth excavator | the dig
Then builders place every block. Start one on its blueprint's home with a supply chest below and an empty chest above.
- builder stack | one hive
- builder fields | the hall
Schematics for Create are in the game's schematics folder as core_*.nbt.
