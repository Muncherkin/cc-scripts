# The Clockworks
> Works beneath the
> factory
Riveted hives of berthed turtles stand in a great hall 60 blocks down, piped to spines of modems and chests. Factory Blocks only: rust, iron, brass and gears.

## Places
- The Engine Hall | 49 wide
- Hives A-D | 32 berths each
- The Mainspring | gear tower
- The Bridge | command room
- The Ledger Room | storage
- The Hatchery | births
- The Conservatory | tree bay
- The Lift | turtle-9 elevator
- The Pneumatic Gate | ore in
- The Pattern Shop | chisels

## Hive, one deck
```
   ##R#R##
  #P  .  P#
 #  GcGcG  #
 R  cKCKc  R
 #  GcGcG  #
  #P  .  P#
   ##R#R##
```
P berth (a turtle), C chest spine, K pipes, c cable, G grate, R rib.

## Costs
- 148 Iron Ingot | one Hive
- 1316 Iron Ingot | the whole works
4 iron + 4 stone make 32 Factory Blocks.

## How it gets built
The builder turtle digs out a blueprint and places every block. Start it on the blueprint's home with a supply chest below and an empty chest above.
- builder stack | one hive
- builder fields | the hall
Schematics for Create are in the game's schematics folder as core_*.nbt.

## Phases
- Pattern Shop or Mason Table
- Dig the Engine Hall
- Hive A, the Mainspring
- The Bridge
- The Ledger Room
- The Conservatory
- Hives B-D, the Hatchery

## Light
Factory Blocks give none. Orange panels mark where lamps would go; add light or mobs spawn.
