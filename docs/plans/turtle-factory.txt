# Turtle Factory
> Step 1: CC builds CC
Goal: a base where turtles and computers turn raw blocks into new turtles and CC parts, then boot each new turtle with our agent. CC does the work. Other mods only where vanilla cannot.

## One mining turtle
- 7 Iron Ingot | casing
- 7 Stone | computer
- 1 Redstone | computer
- 1 Glass Pane | computer
- 8 Planks | chest
- 3 Diamond | pickaxe
- 2 Stick | pickaxe
- Fuel | charcoal or coal
No modem needed: the agent talks to the bridge over HTTP.

## Sources (CC first)
Cobble: a turtle mines a water+lava cobble generator forever. Furnaces turn it into stone.
Logs: turtle tree farm, or the Tree Station pots.
Sand: turtles dig desert sand. A crusher fed by turtles only if that runs dry.
Iron, redstone, diamond, gold: miners now, Geo Scanner miner next. Later Mystical Agriculture crops, planted and harvested by a turtle like the wheat farm.
Pearls: later. A mob farm with a sword turtle, or an enderman crop.

## Factory
```
 [chests]==wired net==
  |    |       |
 [furn][furn] [crafty T]
                 |
 [disk drive]-[new T]
```
- Storage | chests on wired modems. The crafty turtle keeps the index.
- Smelting | furnaces on the same net. The turtle feeds them and empties them.
- Crafting | storage pushes a recipe's items into the turtle's grid; it crafts and sends results back.
- Births | it places a new turtle by a disk drive. The disk installs our agent. The turtle joins the bridge and gets fuel.

## How it runs
The factory turtle stores, crafts, smelts and builds new turtles. Newborns boot from the floppy under the birth spot and start their job.
The birth spot is also a fuel station: a turtle parked there gets charcoal.
Sand still comes from you; glass stops when it runs out.
