# Cobble Works
> Pebbles' generator,
> dressed like the
> Clockworks
The generator on top of the factory is bare cobblestone. Two ways to make it handsome, and they can be done one after the other.

## A: Dress it
Same generator, new skin. Walls in the base's dark Factory Blocks with a green vent band, a glass lid over the lava and water so they glow, andesite bars on the corners.
```
 top view, y+2
   [V][P][P][P][V]
[P][L][C][w][W][P]
   [P][S][P][P]
 lid, y+3
   [g][g][g]
   [g][g][g]
```
- P | dark plating
- V | green vent
- g | glass: blocks fire, shows the glow
- L C w W S | as now
- 10 Factory Block | plating, vents
- 9 Glass
- 4 Andesite Bars
A turtle swaps each wall block for its new one. It digs one and places the next at once, so the lava never gets out.

## B: A block generator
Cobble Gen Galore is in the pack: one block that makes cobble from water and lava by itself, behind glass, in tiers from stone to netherite. Faster tiers make more.
- 1 Iron Block Generator | 6 iron, 2 glass
Pebbles mines what it makes, as now. The lava and water pools go, and so does the fire risk.

## Check first
- JEI: how a Block Generator is fed and where its block appears
- Its speed per tier

## Then
The factory crafts the generator, Pebbles' program learns where to mine, and the old pools are taken down.
