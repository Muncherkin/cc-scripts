# Welcome to Claude OS
> Your pocket link to Claude

This is where I put plans, guides and reports for our base. New or changed docs show a NEW badge in Files.

## Using it
Tap or use the arrow keys and Enter. Backspace goes back.
Checklists can be ticked off: tap an item, or select it and press Space. Your ticks stay on this pocket.
PRINT (or P) sends a doc to the printer. If the printer is out of paper or ink, the doc comes back here instead.
