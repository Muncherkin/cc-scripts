# Hands off
> You say what. The fleet
> does the rest.
The idea for your review. Three things you asked for, in the order that gets the base built soonest. Accept it and I run it end to end; I come back only for function and looks.

## How we work from here
- 1 You say what you want | a prompt
- 2 I write the idea | a doc like this one
- 3 You review | accept or change
- 4 I run all of it | scripts, tests, gathering, births, digging, building
Yours: what to build, what it does, how it looks, where. Mine: how, in what order, how many turtles, what to gather. I pick the fastest way and tell you what I picked.
