# Cobble Generator
> Phase 2: built by a newborn
The factory builds a mining turtle, places it in front of itself and boots it from a floppy. The new turtle climbs up and builds a cobble generator on top of the factory, then sits on the input chest and drops cobble into it.

## Side view
Stand behind the factory, looking the way it faces.
```
 y+2  [S][C]
 y+1  [I][#]
 y+0  [F][B]
      [O][D]
```
- F | factory turtle
- I | input chest
- O | output chest
- B | birth spot, in front of F
- D | disk drive, under B
- C | cobble forms here
- S | the new turtle's seat

## Build layer (y+2)
Top view, F's facing is up.
```
   [#][#][#][#][#]
[#][L][C][w][W][#]
   [#][S][#][#]
```
- L | lava source
- w | water falls into a hole below w
- W | water source
- # | wall: placed if empty, kept if solid

## You do
- Disk drive under B | or left/right of B
- Wired modem on the drive | cable to the network, right-click it
- 1 Floppy Disk in the drive | 2 are in the output chest
- 1 Lava Bucket | into the input chest
- 1 Water Bucket | into the input chest
- Keep clear: B, the 2 blocks above B, the block above I
- Keep clear: L, w, W and the hole under w
- No wood near the lava | it can start fires
- Cables low | not through the build

## Then
Claude runs factory birth cobble. The newborn never digs while building. If something is in its way it stops and says where.
