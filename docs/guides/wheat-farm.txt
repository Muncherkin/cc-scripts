# Wheat Farm
> turtle-2, stationary, no fuel
```
      W
    W[T]W     T = turtle
      W       W = wheat
```
Checks the four crops every minute, harvests ripe wheat, bakes bread and drops it in the chest below. A mod replants for it.

## Tips
- Bone Meal | put some in the turtle and it uses it on growing wheat
- Ctrl+T in its shell stops the farm until a reboot
