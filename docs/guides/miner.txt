# Branch Miner
> turtle-1, mining dimension y=-16
```
 ################
 #[o_o]>   $  * #
 ################
```
Digs a main tunnel forward with 16-block branches left and right every 3 blocks. Mines out every ore vein it sees and throws stone away.

## What it needs
- 1 Diamond Pickaxe | brand new, equipped
- 1 Chunk Controller | equipped
- 1 Bucket | scoops lava for fuel
- 16 Coal | or charcoal
- 4 Chest | more = longer runs

## When it stops
It comes home by itself when fuel gets low or it runs out of chests. Full chests are left in the tunnel ceiling.
After refuelling, ask Claude to run: miner continue
