east strip, for now. A
turtle (the Mover) carries
the storage down the
service shaft. Pebbles is
taken down. The ender
drawer stays for you to
reset.

## Rules for the build
- Barrels, not chests | chests side by side join, and show up twice
- Each barrel on one modem | as drawn
- Input, output chest | off the network
- Keep lanes open | to the ceiling
- Right-click 8 modems | and the drive's

## Lanes
Turtles fly in at y 6
(along z first) and out at
y 7 (along x first), down
and up the service shaft;
coming down has right of
way.

Fuel station: in down the
column above B, out north
to o and up. One way only,
so a turtle waiting above
never blocks one leaving.

Deliveries: down the
column at v, north to i,
into the input chest east;
out west to < and up.

The Mover fills barrels
from above: the E row,
then the row on top.

## What moves later
The strip is in the hall's
build. Before builder
fields lays the floor and
walls, the factory moves to
its own rooms: storage to
the Ledger Room racks
(west), birth spot to the
Hatchery (south). That move
needs no carrying: cable
the racks to this network,
factory input all, and the
barrels empty into the
racks. Nothing else in the
plan touches x 21-24 (Hive
D's ring ends at x 20).
