# Turtle target
> 1 turtle a minute: what
> it takes

From Geode's logs: 12.9 h
running, 39 areas. One
Geode-class miner, per hour:
- 44 | diamond ore
- 86 | iron ore
- 269 | coal ore
- 27 | redstone ore (~120 redstone)

## One turtle costs
Mining turtle, plain:
- 7 Iron | the turtle
- 1 Chest | 2 logs
- 1 Computer | 7 stone, redstone, glass
- 3 Diamond | the pickaxe
Chunk controller on top:
- 4 Iron, 4 Redstone
- 1 Ender Pearl | + blaze powder
End Automata Core on top:
- ~6 Iron, 45 Redstone
- 2 Diamond, soul lantern
- 1 Regen potion | wart, tear
- 10 endermen | fed by Ender

## At 60 an hour
Plain miners:
- 420 iron | 5 Geodes
- 180 diamond | 4-5 Geodes
- 120 logs | ~40 pine spots
With chunk controllers:
- 60 ender pearls | no source
With End cores:
- 600 endermen | Ender does
  about 10 an hour
- 60 potions | no source

## So
- Miners stay plain | no pearls, no potions
- Mining chunks force-loaded | FTB Chunks, by you
- 6 Geode-class miners | feed 1 a minute
- Logs are the limit | grow the pine farm
- End cores only on ferries | 1-3 turtles
Ferries warp between the
dimensions with batches:
new miners out, broken
ones back. Items already
cross by ender drawer.

## End cores
Ender keeps feeding: put
Weak cores in its input,
it makes End cores. Each
Weak core needs the long
Regeneration potion, so
it's a few a day at most.
